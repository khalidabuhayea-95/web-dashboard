# Canva Reference Analysis

**Accessed:** 2026-11-05, logged in, via the public template search pages (canva.com/templates/?query=…). For each query, the template IDs, slugs and thumbnails were read from the server-rendered results (first 10 per query). Contact sheets were then rendered and inspected visually. These are the screenshots in `assets/canva-*.jpg`.

**Queries:**
- English: arabic wedding invitation, engagement invitation arabic, ramadan post, eid mubarak post, islamic quote post, birthday invitation, graduation invitation, baby announcement, instagram post sale, product promotion instagram post, restaurant menu, food promotion instagram post, luxury business card, certificate, educational infographic, youtube thumbnail, resume, logo, arabic calligraphy poster, event poster, instagram story, flyer, presentation, facebook cover.
- Arabic-specific: دعوة زفاف، رمضان كريم، عيد مبارك، عيد ميلاد سعيد, arabic instagram post / menu / certificate / business card / youtube thumbnail / flyer / sale post / graduation / infographic / logo.

## Headline finding: Canva is weak in Arabic
- **Arabic queries (دعوة زفاف، عيد ميلاد سعيد):** these returned mostly English templates. «عيد ميلاد سعيد» returned Eid and UAE National Day templates, not birthday ones. Arabic search relevance is poor.
- **"Arabic wedding invitation" results:** about 9 of 10 are English body text with only an Arabic بسملة or a calligraphy accent on top (e.g. EAHHMYGKCQU, EAHHAO4BqCQ, EAG28Sb7K14, EAHHT3VxuVk).
- **Arabic YouTube thumbnail / business card / infographic:** essentially no Arabic-language results; these are filled with hijaiyah flashcards and English templates.
- **Opportunity:** full Arabic-first templates in these categories have little competition on Canva.

## What Canva does well (borrow the principles)
| Category | Observed pattern | Example IDs |
|---|---|---|
| Wedding (Arabic-flavoured) | - Ornamental full-bleed backgrounds (damask, etched floral, Islamic arch)<br>- Centred script names<br>- Tiny body text<br>- Small بسملة at the top as a crown | EAHHMYGKCQU pink-cream traditional; EAHHAYu_JBQ black-gold; EAG7tB_KITY green Islamic; EAG28Sb7K14 navy-gold Islamic arch |
| Ramadan/Eid | Navy+gold mosque silhouettes; orange monoline mosque; Arabic calligraphy as hero with English sub; strong brand-ad usage (Iftar specials) | "gold-navy-blue-minimalist-elegant-ramadan", "ramadan-mubarak-muslim-fasting-month" |
| Sale / product | - Giant typographic SALE / % numerals<br>- Product cut-out with shadow on a solid colour<br>- 3D podiums<br>- One accent colour | "orange-bold-sale-instagram-post", "blue-minimalist-product-promotion", "neutral-minimalist-product-promotion" |
| Food | Food photo as hero on a saturated background (red/yellow); condensed bold headline; price badge circle; "order now" pill | "black-red-minimalist-food-promotion-burger", "red-gradient-vibrant-food-promotion" |
| Certificates | Gold ornamental corners, centred serif, Islamic marriage certificate variants | "gold-islamic-traditional-elegant-wedding-certificate" |
| Logos | Monogram in ornament; mosque/dome icon + caps wordmark | "arabic-neutral-elegant-wedding-logo", "green-illustration-minimalist-mosque-logo" |

## Weaknesses to avoid copying
- Arabic used as decoration over English-first layouts.
- Small Arabic body text sizes.
- Generic Arabic fonts rather than calligraphy for the hero.
- Repetitive mosque/lantern clichés.
