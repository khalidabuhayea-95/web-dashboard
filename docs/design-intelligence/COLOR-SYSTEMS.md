# Color Systems

Rule: dominant (60%) + secondary (30%) + accent (10%). Text colour must pass contrast against its actual background, so check on the photo, not the swatch.

| Green | Islam, freshness, nature |

| Burgundy | passion, luxury |

## Gradients and metallics
- Gold must not be a flat #FFD700. Use a 3-stop gradient (#8A6A2B → #E9CF8A → #9C7A34), or a Magnific-made foil texture placed inside the text or shape.
- Gradients: 2 stops of neighbouring hues. Avoid purple→pink "AI" gradients unless the brief is explicitly Gen-Z.
