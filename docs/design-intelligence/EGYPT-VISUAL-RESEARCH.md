# Egypt category: visual research (2026-11-12)

Ali rejected batch EG1's research («التغذية البصرية سيئة»). Canva and Andalusi are weak for Egypt. This file is the analysis of about 80 designs studied one by one on Pinterest (Ali's login, Claude browser) plus Behance. Read it before building any Egypt design.

## Where to look
Pinterest keywords that returned real Egyptian work: «تصميم سوشيال ميديا مصر وطني», «6 أكتوبر تصميم بوستر مصر», «عيد تحرير سيناء تصميم», «ثورة 30 يونيو 23 يوليو تصميم تهنئة», «المتحف المصري الكبير تصميم سوشيال ميديا», «تصميم إعلان مصري فرعوني سوشيال ميديا», «Egypt social media design arabic», «Egypt nostalgia poster design مصر زمان», «تصميم سوشيال ميديا مطعم مصري كشري عروض».
Useless: «نصر أكتوبر» alone (returns Al Nassr football), «مصر أم الدنيا تصميم» (religious quotes), «إطار صورة شخصية علم مصر» (generic black-gold game frames).
Behance: «نصر أكتوبر تصميم», «تحيا مصر». Freepik is weak. To study pins at a readable size, collect the 236x image URLs from the results page, swap to 736x and show three per screen in a fixed overlay.

## Why EG1 failed
Every EG1 design was "calligraphy lockup on a flat texture plus one cut-out object". That is the Jordan formula. Egyptian designers almost never do that. Their designs have a scene or a concept, the title lives inside the picture, and the flag is a moving ribbon, not a sticker. EG1's palette (sand, lapis, turquoise) also missed: the real palette is flag red, white and black with gold, on warm sunset or clean white.

## What the references have in common
1. One idea per design. The picture tells it before the text: an old man holding a miniature Egypt in his hands, a pyramid with a doorway of light, a flag cloth lying like a dune in front of the pyramids, a view of the flag from inside a cave.
2. The flag is cloth. It flows as a ribbon through the scene, wraps a statue, drapes over an object, hangs as a curtain across the top, or is planted by a silhouette. A flat rectangular flag appears only in minimal greetings.
3. Depth. Foreground object, hero, background, haze and one warm light. Text sits in the sky or on a calm band.
4. A big number or date is often the hero: «6», «25», «51», «1973». It is 3D gold or stone, filled with a photo, or flat red with a flag stripe wrapped around it.
5. Title type has two modes. Formal: thuluth or diwani in black with one red word, or white on a dark sky. Modern: very heavy geometric or hand-drawn display Arabic, sometimes extruded 3D with a shadow. Small text is a light spaced sans. An English line often sits under the Arabic title.
6. A greeting from a business has a fixed skeleton: logo top, title, hero, «كل عام وأنتم بخير», footer with phone and site.

## Style families (with what makes each work)
A. Cinematic scene. Pyramids, Nile and golden light; flag ribbon through the frame; title inside the scene («هبة النيل» sits between the pyramids with a sun flare behind it); 3D gold «EGYPT» letters; past and present split by a glowing Nile curve; person seen from behind in a flag jacket.
B. Clean national greeting. White or light grey marble; faint eagle watermark; red number with flag stripe; small black silhouettes of soldiers and planes; thuluth title; a pyramid-shaped photo mask; red tab at the top holding the logo.
C. Vintage and collage. Illustrated old map with an ornamental border, compass, coffee pot and dates; giant postage stamp as a diorama with a tiny figure for scale; kraft-paper scrapbook for a city (taped name label, stamps, newspaper clipping, tea seller, old Fiat, tram): this is a governorates series (القاهرة، الإسكندرية، سوهاج); newspaper background with heavy red type; ink sketch with one red sun disc carrying the date; engraved two-colour poster with an icon row; old classified newspaper ad with a price box.
D. Carved and material. Whole scene as a white marble relief with only the flag in colour; carved wooden frame with a gold nameplate; satellite terrain in grey with a coloured flag.
E. Pharaonic. Gold mask or statue hero on warm brown or deep purple and orange (GEM style) with numbered facts; papyrus page with columns and winged sun for a product; product dressed as a pharaoh (tooth in a nemes, bottle in a sarcophagus niche with a white sticker outline); the "what if the pharaohs had social media" joke series.
F. Street and pop. Yellow or blue flat background, huge bubbly hand lettering with doodles over a street scene (tuk-tuk, cat, felucca); halftone cut-outs (Umm Kulthum, old taxi, downtown balconies, Cairo tower); sticker lettering «حلوة يا بلدي»; real Upper-Egypt characters in editorial ads.
G. Food and offers. Retro sunburst rays, extruded 3D title with a hard shadow, giant product over a Cairo skyline; red and orange; hand holding the product; cut-out character presenting the dish.
H. Clean modern system (Saudi camel festival series, useful as a method): cream background, one subject, coloured silk ribbons flowing around it, big friendly display type, tiny diamond confetti. In flag colours this suits Egypt.

## Ideas that fit our rules directly
- Number-shaped Canva frames: the digits of a date hold the replaceable photo (seen on «25» for Sinai). A real frame, editable, and distinctive.
- Blank "decree" frame: flag curtain across the top with the eagle, parchment centre with light rays, pyramids and golden Nile at the bottom, flag ribbons rising from the bottom corners. Text and name go in the centre.
- Governorates scrapbook series, one city per design.
- Giant stamp diorama; old newspaper ad for offers; marble relief greeting; cave-opening view; miniature Egypt in hands.
- Personal greeting without a photo: name and title as a signature line under the greeting (doctor, shop, office).
- Portrait designs: build from families B, C and F (stamp frame, scrapbook photo with tape, number frame, clean white with ribbon). The Pinterest "Egypt photo frame" results are generic and not Egyptian.

## Constraints to keep
No black backgrounds (use deep brown, navy or purple-orange for family E). No arch or dome portrait frames (the carved-arch window reference is a scene window, not a portrait slot). No text baked into generated art except calligraphy lockups; titles that must sit inside a scene are generated as lockups or 3D word art and placed as separate layers. Flags from the Canva library, or flag cloth generated as part of a scene only if Ali allows it (ask: the references rely on cloth flags inside the scene).
