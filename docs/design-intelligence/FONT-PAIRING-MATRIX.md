## Pairing logic
- **Contrast by class, not by size alone:** pair a calligraphic or Naskh hero with a geometric sans body, or a Kufi headline with a Naskh body.
- **Shared proportions:** Tajawal, Alexandria and Cairo all have short ascenders, so they sit well under tall Thuluth.
- **One personality per design:** Lalezar and Thuluth never go together.
- **Weights:**
  - Body: Light/Regular.
  - Info labels: Medium.
  - Headlines: Bold/Black.
  - Never use more than three weights.
- **English accent (bilingual):**
  - Classic designs: Cormorant / Playfair italic.
  - Modern designs: Montserrat / Poppins.
  - The Arabic line always leads, and the English sits smaller underneath.
