# Design Quality Checklist

Score every design from an exported image (not from memory). The pass mark is **85/100**, AND it must have zero auto-reject items.

| Criterion | Weight | 100% looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| Visual composition | 20 | One dominant element, clear Z/axis flow, margins respected, balanced |
| Typography | 20 | Correct pairing, ≥3× scale contrast, legible body, no tofu or broken joins, RTL correct |
| Color harmony | 15 | 60/30/10, contrast passes, palette matches the occasion |
| Creativity / originality | 15 | Recognisable concept, not a copy, not generic AI |
| Professional polish | 10 | Alignment exact, consistent spacing, no stray elements, crisp assets |
| Category relevance | 10 | Right format and phrases, all required info slots present |
| Editability / usability | 10 | Text editable, elements separated, photo frames real, layers named sensibly |

## Auto-reject (score is irrelevant)
- Incorrect Arabic text (spelling, wrong verse, reversed or isolated letters)
- Broken Arabic shaping or tofu boxes (e.g. Reem Kufi with ٪)
- Unbalanced composition, or text cropped or touching the edge
- Body text unreadable at phone size, or contrast < 4.5:1
- Inappropriate type (calligraphy used for paragraphs, comic font for condolences)
- Garbled generated text inside images
- Low-resolution or pixelated assets, or watermarks
- Mixed styles inside one design
- Non-editable text when the job is a template
- Assets without usage rights

## Review procedure
1. Export a PNG or thumbnail (Canva `export-design`, or the read-design thumbnail) and look at it at 100% and at phone size.
2. Run the 4 balance checks (COMPOSITION-RULES).
3. Score each criterion, write one sentence of evidence for each, and total it.
4. If it fails, list the concrete fixes, apply them, then re-export and re-score.
5. Log it in DESIGN-REVIEW-LOG.md. Never approve your own work without the exported image in view.
