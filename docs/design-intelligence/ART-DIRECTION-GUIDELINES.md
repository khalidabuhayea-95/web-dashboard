# Art Direction Guidelines

## Direction card (fill it in before touching Canva)
```
Brief: <category> · <audience> · <platform/size> · <language>
Concept (1 line): what idea or feeling carries the design
Mood words (3): e.g. hushed, golden, ceremonial
Style: <VISUAL-STYLE-LIBRARY #>
Palette: <COLOR-SYSTEMS name> (+ accent)
Type: hero / headline / body (FONT-PAIRING-MATRIX)
Layout: <COMPOSITION code>
Hero element: what the eye hits first
Supporting: max 2 decorative families
Background: flat / gradient / texture / Magnific scene
References: ≥5 (file + note on what is borrowed: composition / palette / type)
Risks: e.g. busy background vs. body text → panel at 0.8 opacity
```

## Creating 3 genuinely different directions
- **Vary at least 3 axes:** layout code, style, palette family, hero type (calligraphy / object / photo / numeral).
- **Example for a wedding:**
  - Direction 1 is Luxury (A + Midnight Gold + Thuluth).
  - Direction 2 is Floral (C + Blush + Diwani + 3D roses).
  - Direction 3 is Minimal Contemporary (G + Sage Linen + Reem Kufi).

## Writing Arabic copy
- Use the category's established phrases. They are what buyers search for and recognise:
  - **Wedding:** بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم · «وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُمْ...» · بكل الحب والسعادة ندعوكم · بحضوركم تكتمل فرحتنا
  - **Graduation:** مبارك التخرج · وأخيراً تخرجت · الحمد لله الذي بنعمته تتم الصالحات
  - **Ramadan:** رمضان كريم · رمضان مبارك · أهلاً رمضان
  - **Eid:** عيد مبارك · عيدكم سعيد · كل عام وأنتم بخير
  - **Sale:** عرض خاص · تخفيضات · لفترة محدودة · تسوق الآن · الجمعة البيضاء
- Placeholder names should be realistic and neutral: أحمد & سارة, محمد & نور.
- Placeholder dates use the future (2030) as Andalusi does, so they are clearly samples.
- Quran, hadith or dua text must be copied exactly from a reliable source, with the reference written beneath. Never paraphrase it.

## What makes AR templates feel "premium" (synthesis of Canva + Andalusi)
1. A calligraphic lockup as the hero, not a typed font at a big size.
2. Photoreal, softly lit 3D materials: satin, roses, pearls, velvet, gold leaf.
3. A very small, airy body text block (light weight, wide line height).
4. A single warm light source and a vignette.
5. Gold used as an accent line or foil, never as a fill on everything.
6. Restraint: Andalusi rarely uses more than 2 ornaments.

## Anti-slop list (reject on sight)
- Purple-pink gradient on white.
- Everything centred with equal sizes.
- Stock "AI glow".
- Generic sans for the hero.
- Ornaments on all four sides plus a pattern plus sparkles.
- English-first layout with Arabic squeezed in.
- Text rendered inside generated images.
