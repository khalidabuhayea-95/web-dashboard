## القرارات الخلافية

Where all three agreed I did not touch anything: avatars-art in the shop window, birthday top-three, the four merchant shelves (shops-offers, food-drink, fashion-jewellery, health-services) at the floor with health-services last, and the faith-national rename.

faith-national at 1 (demand said 3, seasonal said 1, firstrun said 6). I took seasonal's slot. Firstrun's objection is not about the category's merit — it says outright that the calendar argues for it and recommends lifting it to position 2 for the 15-25 September window; its complaint is that the strip opens on Ramadan suhoor, eidiya and white ihram. That is an internal sortOrder problem, so I fixed it internally instead of paying for it with the category slot: faith-watan-glory to position 0, founding-day-palms to the tail, jumaa-morning to 1. This slot ships only with that internal reorder plus the rename. It is also not a pure seasonal bet — جمعة مباركة fires every Friday, so the shelf keeps a floor after the 24th, which is why I was willing to spend position 1 on it.

birthday at 2, not 1 or 0. Demand and seasonal both ranked it 2 and I kept it there. It loses nothing permanent by sitting behind a category with a hard 17-day deadline, and it takes position 0 the morning of 24 September. I would not let it fall below 3 under any argument: it is the only shelf in the top four with women, children and a baby in it, and rows 0 and 3 have no woman-ref card at all.

friends-lifestyle at 3 (demand said 7, seasonal 3, firstrun 2). Demand ranked it off the title, which is wrong on the contents — there are no friends in it. It carries two things nothing else does this week: the life-journey transform block, which is the strongest top-of-funnel card in the app, and the only two back-to-school cards, in the week school starts. Also move first-day-school out of graduation (it is buried at position 14 of 15 in a category called التخرج, where no parent will look for it) into friends-lifestyle at position 0. That single template move is worth more than any category swap below rank 4.

travel-seasons at 4 (seasonal said 9, firstrun 4). Seasonal's trough argument only applies to the eight weather cards; the destination and heritage blocks are aseasonal, and heritage is the block users hunt during National Day week. Resolved by ordering inside the strip — destinations and heritage first, weather last — rather than by demoting the shelf.

style-beauty at 5, not 4 (demand). Demand itself concedes 13 of 16 are man-only. Against a heavily female audience I will not put a men's luxury aisle above the wow shelf, and its name currently sends women looking for beauty into it.

family-weddings 6 above graduation 7. Effectively a tie across the three lenses; broken on the fact that family's newborn/new-home/nikah core is aseasonal while graduation is deep post-peak and has just lost its only in-window card.

food-drink 10 above fashion-jewellery 11. Demand ranked food dead last, the other two ranked it above fashion. Two of three plus the nearer rebound (winter cup, kunafa, Arabic coffee in six to ten weeks vs Eid in five months) decides it.

Renames I am shipping: faith-national "مناسبات دينية" → "مناسبات دينية ووطنية" (all three asked for it, and it is the most expensive mismatch in the app this week); travel-seasons → "السفر والتراث والأجواء" (two proposed it identically); health-services "بشرة وعطور" → "العطور والعناية" (all three want perfume to lead, forty of fifty-three cards are perfume staging); style-beauty "الستايل والجمال" → "الأناقة والفخامة" (drops a beauty promise backed by exactly one card, and the transliteration); shops-offers → "متجري: منتجات وعروض" so non-merchants self-select out instead of tapping into nineteen podiums; friends-lifestyle → "رحلة العمر" — the three lenses proposed three different names but all agreed the current one is false, so I took the one that names the actual hook with no transliteration and no promise of friends or gyms. I am deferring the family-weddings and career-business renames: only one lens asked, and neither title is actively lying about its contents.

## المخاطر والمتابعة

WATCH FIRST WEEK
Row 0 is 27 cards of ref=man. If the picker asks a hijab-wearing woman for "a man's photo" on the very first row of the app, we tell half the audience the shelf is not for them before they ever tap. Re-tag the identity-agnostic cards (3d-avatar, colour-avatar, classical-oil, watercolour, pop-art, black-and-white, arabesque-mosaic, magazine-cover) to a neutral ref before this order ships. If that cannot land this week, that is the one and only reason to swap birthday into position 0 instead.
The national bet rests on a single card, faith-watan-glory, and it is ref=man. Watch its conversion daily from 15 September. The fix is content, not sort: commission a woman's and a child's National Day card this week, and a green-and-flag avatar for row 0 — an avatar shelf is exactly where a National Day profile picture gets made, and right now that entire surge converts to nothing.
Verify before shipping: life-journey-me-and-younger-me and life-journey-childhood-era are tagged ref=child but their prompts preserve the adult face. If the uploader asks for a child's photo, the poster card of position 3 misfires on first contact.
Two trust risks a sort order cannot fix, both in family-weddings: weddings-elegant-nikah renders a gilded Quran, and AI Arabic script on a mushaf comes out as gibberish — pull the prop now; and weddings-white-rose-arch and weddings-garden-gown lack the modest-and-elegant constraint the other women's cards carry, so a 100-credit gown card can return bare shoulders. That is a refund and a trust problem, not a styling note.
Pricing shape: birthday and graduation are 100% 175-credit cards, so the two highest occasion shelves have no cheap entry point anywhere. Add one 100-credit text-free card to each and put it in the first four.
Watch bounce on friends-lifestyle if the gym block is not pushed to the tail — three of the six gym cards are explicitly dark or muted and will read as near-black at thumbnail size.

faith-national drops from 1 to about 8 the morning of 24 September, keeping only جمعة مباركة; birthday takes position 0 and holds it. career-business gets a two-week lift to 4 for Teacher's Day on 5 October — float teachers-day to position 0 of that strip by 20 September, it is also the only woman card in a nineteen-card shelf. travel-seasons holds 4 and its weather tail finally earns its place with the first Gulf rains: bring first-rain, seasons-rainy-day and seasons-morning-fog forward inside the strip. graduation slides to 9. friends-lifestyle returns the back-to-school cards to its tail once term settles, keeping the life-journey block at the head.

**لماذا الأوائل:** Position 0 is birthday-candle-glow because it is the archetype of the whole category: a face behind a cake with lit candles and sparklers. Nothing here is more universally wanted, and the scene is the strongest thumbnail in the set — glowing candles and sparkler light against a dark cinematic ground is maximum contrast on a small phone tile, with one clear subject. Its phrase "كل عام وأنت بخير" is the single most-used Arabic birthday greeting in the Gulf, so the poster reads instantly as "birthday" without the user parsing the art.

Positions 1-3 are chosen to interleave the upload type so every kind of user sees themselves in the first row: woman, child, baby. birthday-foil-balloons at 1 is the bright counterweight to the dark poster — a blush pink and cream balloon wall is the most legible colour block in the category and its "elegant modest outfit" framing speaks directly to the core Gulf-women audience; its "عيد ميلاد سعيد" is the neutral phrase anyone can use. birthday-kids-wonder at 2 captures the parent segment, which is the second-biggest buyer of birthday content, and giant colourful balloons plus confetti and fairy lights is the most saturated thumbnail available. birthday-first-birthday at 3 is narrow on ref but has the highest emotional payoff and share rate of anything on the shelf — first-birthday smash-cake shoots are a genre Gulf mothers post as a matter of course — and it completes the man/woman/child/baby sweep across the opening row.

birthday-cartoon-me at 4 is the freshness slot: a 3D animated-movie version of yourself is the one card here a user has not seen in ten other Arabic photo apps, and "سنة حلوة يا أنا" is a self-celebration line built for posting. birthday-golden-gala at 6... at 5 follows as the rich-colour glamour card (burgundy velvet, gold, tiered cake) for the female audience that just met a cartoon.

Note: the cost-mixing rule cannot be honoured — every one of the 15 birthday cards is 175 credits. Flagged below.

**لماذا الأواخر:** birthday-new-year-of-me (11) has a genuinely fresh idea — "كل عام وأنا بخير", celebrating your own year — but the art is beige-on-cream minimal, which is the lowest-contrast tile in the category and will disappear in a scrolling strip. It is held out of the bottom three only because the concept is good; it belongs where a user who is already browsing deliberately will find it.

birthday-sparkle-his (12) is a straight gendered clone of birthday-sparkle-night: same sparkler candle, same moody upscale lounge, same phrase inflected he/she. One of the pair earns a high slot; the second is a near-duplicate and a man in a dark blazer in a dark lounge is a muted thumbnail on top of that.

birthday-royal-frame (13) is the third child card and the weaker half of the ornate-gold pair with birthday-golden-throne. A child standing inside a baroque picture frame on a pastel stage is also conceptually confusing at thumbnail size — the eye cannot tell whether it is a portrait, a prop, or a real scene, and cluttered ornament loses at 120px.

birthday-elegant-dinner (14) is last. It reads as a restaurant photo, not a birthday card: a seated figure at a set table, muted upscale palette, cake competing with tableware. It duplicates the "seated with a cake in a tasteful room" idea already served better by birthday-golden-gala and birthday-new-year-of-me, and its phrase "كل عام وأنتِ بخير" is the feminine twin of the poster card's line, so it adds no new greeting either.

Nothing was demoted for season — birthdays are year-round, so this shelf holds its shape through the National Day and back-to-school peak. That is also its weakness: see the flags.

**لماذا الأوائل:** Every one of the 27 cards is ref=man, cost=100, text="" — so the interleave-the-ref and mix-the-price rules cannot be satisfied by ordering (see flags; this is a data defect, not an ordering choice). Ordering therefore rests entirely on subject matter, and the first five deliberately alternate across the four visual families (3D / painterly / flat-graphic / anime / editorial) so no two adjacent cards read as the same treatment at thumbnail size.

0 social-avatars-3d-avatar — the shelf poster. A polished 3D avatar is the single most universally wanted output in this whole category: it is the profile picture people came here for, it works for any age or gender, it survives a hijab or a beard without the model fighting the reference, and glossy 3D is the most legible thing that exists at 120px on a phone. Highest breadth plus highest thumbnail pull.

1 fine-art-classical-oil — the "wow, that's art" card and the one people actually forward. Deep chiaroscuro and rich impasto give a dark, saturated, unmistakable thumbnail that contrasts hard against card 0, and an old-master portrait carries prestige across every age bracket, including the older users who will not touch anime.

2 social-avatars-colour-avatar — the workhorse. Bold flat colour, one clear subject, zero styling risk; this is the card a hesitant first-time user taps because they can predict the result. It is not fresh, which is why it sits behind the two showpieces rather than at 0, but its breadth earns row one.

3 gaming-anime-anime-transform — the highest-volume request in the young Gulf segment and the card most likely to be screenshotted into a group chat. Placed fourth so the top row reaches young users without letting anime lead a shelf whose audience is mixed-age.

4 posters-magazine-cover — the first card in the opening that reads as glamour rather than avatar: gel-lit colour background, direct confident gaze, high-fashion styling. It is the strongest pull for women in the top row and its saturated background block is one of the most legible thumbnails in the set.

Just behind them, fine-art-arabesque-mosaic at 5 is the freshness play — muted gold and deep teal Islamic mosaic is the one treatment here that users have not already seen in ten other apps, and it reads as made-for-this-audience rather than translated.

**لماذا الأواخر:** 23 posters-the-seventies — a deliberately faded, low-contrast, halftone-textured palette is the worst possible thumbnail on a bright phone screen, and 1970s American film-poster nostalgia has no purchase with a Saudi or Levantine audience; the reference is borrowed, not felt.

24 gaming-anime-pixel-world — structurally broken against its own promise. The prompt swears the face stays unmistakably the same person, then renders it in retro pixel art, which destroys facial likeness by definition; at thumbnail size a user cannot tell whose face it is. Narrow retro-gamer taste on top of that.

25 fine-art-lithograph — vintage engraving is an art-history taste, not a mass one; it is monochrome and fine-lined, so it reads as grey mush in a card strip, and its Arabic title (الطباعة الحجرية) is a print-shop term most browsers will not decode.

26 posters-business-cover — the narrowest card on the shelf. It presumes formal attire and a professional self-image, so it excludes students, homemakers, children and anyone photographed in casual or traditional dress, and it is a duller near-duplicate of posters-magazine-cover at position 4 — same editorial-cover idea, solid background, no glamour payoff.

Also pushed down: fine-art-colour-abstract (21) for the same self-defeating reason as pixel-world — abstract expressive treatment fights the "identity 100% identical" instruction, so the output is unpredictable; gaming-anime-legendary-knight (22) as niche fantasy-RPG taste; gaming-anime-esports-avatar (20) and gaming-anime-player-card (19) because both demand a specific hobby identity (gamer, footballer) and player-card is a weaker echo of posters-sports-poster; social-avatars-patterned-backdrop (18) because it is the subtle, muted version of fine-art-arabesque-mosaic already sitting at position 5.

**لماذا الأواخر:** The bottom five are the gym block minus its one broad card, and they lose on every criterion that matters.

gym-fitness-strength-training (9) and gym-fitness-boxing (12) are dark low-key scenes with rim light and dust particles — they turn to mud in a bright scrolling grid on a phone, and both read as male-only gym-goer content.

gym-fitness-before-and-after (10) is a split frame: two small figures at thumbnail size, nothing legible, and the promise ("your body transformed") is one many users will find unflattering rather than fun.

gym-fitness-calm-yoga (11) is explicitly muted — sage tones, soft curtain light, minimal studio — which is the definition of a thumbnail that loses, and it is tagged ref=man for a scene that reads female.

gym-fitness-crossfit (13) is last because it is a near-duplicate of strength-training (barbell/kettlebell, industrial gym, dramatic side light) with a narrower, more jargon-heavy hook; it is the most niche card on the shelf.

winter-camp-night is only at 8 rather than the very bottom on purpose: it is out of season (camping starts November) and the only 175-credit card, but it is by far the most culturally native and freshest image here — it belongs mid-shelf now and in the top three from 1 November.
